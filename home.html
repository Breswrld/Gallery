Welcome to Digital Imaging II: Adobe Photoshop

For this project, I focused on pushing the limits of digital collage and image manipulation in Photoshop by carefully integrating custom elements, precise masking, and advanced blending techniques. My goal was to create a seamless, cohesive composition that transforms the original source material into something entirely new, paying close attention to lighting, scale, and texture to ensure the illusion remains completely believable.
